# Safety and privacy principles

Orion-Core is designed as a diagnostic and research tool.

- Default behavior is read-only. It must not change system settings, registry values, services, drivers, or security controls.
- Do not collect usernames, hostnames, MAC addresses, serial numbers, product keys, personal files, or other unnecessary identifiers.
- Do not execute downloaded or otherwise untrusted binaries.
- Do not bypass DRM, anti-cheat, licensing, platform security, or access controls.
- Any future active test must be clearly documented, opt-in, narrowly scoped, and reversible where applicable.
- Reports should disclose what was collected and allow the user to review data before sharing it.

If a proposed feature conflicts with these principles, safety and user control take priority.
